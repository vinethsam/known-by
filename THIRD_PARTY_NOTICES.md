## Liquid Glass JS

`src/glass/liquidGlassRenderer.js` adapts the shape masking, edge/rim refraction,
background sampling, and blur approach from [Liquid Glass JS](https://github.com/dashersw/liquid-glass-js)
by Armagan Amcalar. The original JavaScript was modified for a React-owned DOM,
the existing KnownBy palette, smaller texture uploads, and explicit resource cleanup.
